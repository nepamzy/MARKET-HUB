# MARKET HUB — APPROVED DECISIONS

Focus: commerce + supply chain + logistics.
No distributors.
No customer-to-customer marketplace.
B2B and B2C both supported.
Direct businesses can procure upstream and serve customers.
Third-party logistics network preferred.
Delivery is optional for customers.
Human-readable address; no manual lat/long.
Main Paystack account + supported seller subaccounts/settlement.
No homemade wallet/escrow.
MARKET HUB pays Paystack processing fees.
Commission tiers: 0.75%, 1.5%, 3%, 2%, 1%, 0.5%.
Commission basis: seller goods subtotal; delivery excluded.
Multi-seller checkout = independent seller orders.
Private pricing/terms are relationship and permission controlled.
Responsive single application.
Professional restrained navy/green/gold visual identity.
Modular monolith initially.
Phase-by-phase development with ChatGPT audit gates.
